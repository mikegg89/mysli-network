Privacy request

Account deletion

To request deletion of your MySLI account, email privacy@mysli.network from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete MySLI Account".

What to include

  • Your account email address.
  • Your role, if known: Deaf user, interpreter, corporate user, or both.
  • A short confirmation that you want your account deleted.

Records we may need to retain

Some records may be retained when required for legal, safety, tax, payment, audit, fraud-prevention, dispute, or compliance reasons. We will minimize retained data where possible.
