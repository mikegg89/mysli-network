Support

MySLI is currently in invite-only pilot and TestFlight preparation. Use the contacts below for the right kind of help.

Contact paths

  • General support: support@mysli.network
  • Privacy and deletion: privacy@mysli.network
  • Safety or serious conduct concerns: safety@mysli.network
  • Billing, receipts, or refunds: billing@mysli.network
  • Interpreter credentials: credentials@mysli.network
  • Accessibility feedback: accessibility@mysli.network

Support privacy

Please include the session date, approximate time, and a short description. Do not include medical diagnosis, treatment details, insurance IDs, full identity document numbers, or unnecessary private health information.
