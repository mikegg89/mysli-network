Interpreter supply

Bring expert interpreting to the moments that need it most.

MySLI is looking for qualified ASL interpreters who care about professional standards, state-specific eligibility, specialized vocabulary, and a better experience for Deaf users.

The best interpreters should have control over rates, availability, specialties, and the kinds of sessions they are prepared to accept. MySLI is built to respect that while keeping compliance gates strict.

Why join the pilot

Specialized matching

Tag specialties like medical, legal, workplace, education, technical, engineering, CAD, and spatial collaboration so the right users can find the right expertise.

Rate and availability control

Set your base rate, corporate preferred rate if you want one, weekly availability, blackout dates, and buffer time between sessions.

Foundation impact

MySLI is designed so 50% of net platform profits support The John Claire Woolsey Foundation, helping fund Deaf family grants, learning grants, and app credits.

What review includes

  • Identity verification before live paid sessions.
  • RID certification and state-specific license or registration document upload where required.
  • State eligibility checks based on where the Deaf user is located.
  • Professional standards and pre-call readiness acknowledgement.
  • Payout setup through Stripe Connect before paid work.
  • Optional VR/AR and CAD collaboration readiness for technical pilots.

Professional standard

MySLI interpreters should accept calls from a private, stable setting with clear lighting, a clean background, visible signing space, and readiness for the session category. Public multitasking, driving, shopping, or unclear video presentation should not be part of paid interpreting work.
