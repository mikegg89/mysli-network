What MySLI is building

MySLI focuses on human interpreter access, not automated sign-language interpretation. Deaf users choose the interpreter whenever possible, and regulated sessions must pass state-specific qualification checks before booking, payment, video launch, or payout.

For Deaf users

Find qualified interpreters for scheduled or urgent video sessions, with specialty matching for work, education, medical, legal, and technical conversations.

For interpreters

Manage profile, credentials, rates, availability, sessions, earnings, and professional standards from a native app experience.

For organizations

Support ADA-required interpreting through corporate payer accounts, QR codes, receipts, and patient-respectful billing flows where the Deaf patient is not charged.

Launch safety promise
  • No AI interpretation.
  • No call recordings.
  • No transcripts.

Community impact

Built to help Deaf families beyond each call.

MySLI is designed so 50% of net platform profits support The John Claire Woolsey Foundation. Corporate-paid sessions can help fund Deaf family grants, learning grants, and shareable app credits that reduce everyday interpreting costs.

Grant amounts are driven by quarterly fund size, not by requester selection, so the process stays transparent and fair as the treasury grows.
