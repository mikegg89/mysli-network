Privacy Policy

This launch policy is prepared for pilot and App Store readiness. It should be reviewed by counsel before broad public release or regulated medical/corporate marketing.

What MySLI does

MySLI connects Deaf users with human sign language interpreters for scheduled and live video interpreting sessions. MySLI does not provide automated sign-language interpretation and does not record video calls in the launch version.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, role, and sign-in status.
  • Profile information for Deaf users, interpreters, and corporate users.
  • Interpreter credential and eligibility information needed for state-specific qualification review.
  • Booking, payment, receipt, support, safety, and audit information needed to operate the service.
  • Approximate or verified location evidence when required for state eligibility, interpreter jurisdiction, or corporate session routing.

Video sessions

MySLI uses a hosted video provider for live interpreting sessions. We do not store call recordings or transcripts in the launch version. Future recording, transcription, or AI-assisted features would require explicit policy, consent, and product updates.

Payments and corporate billing

MySLI may use Stripe to process payments, support connected interpreter payouts, and manage corporate billing. Payment processors receive information needed to complete payment, payout, refund, and receipt workflows.

Support and safety

Users can contact support or report safety concerns. Please do not include medical diagnosis, treatment details, insurance IDs, or unnecessary private health information in email support requests.
