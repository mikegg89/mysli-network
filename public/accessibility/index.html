Accessibility

Accessibility commitment

MySLI is being built for the Deaf community first. The app and website should be usable with large text, high contrast, keyboard navigation, screen readers, captions where appropriate, and clear visual states.

What we are prioritizing

  • VoiceOver-friendly labels and clear controls in the iOS app.
  • High contrast, readable type, and large touch targets.
  • Human interpreter access and specialty matching.
  • Respectful support paths for Deaf users, interpreters, and organizations.

Report an accessibility issue

Email accessibility@mysli.network with the page, device, browser or app version, and what did not work. We welcome direct, practical feedback.
