Terms of Service

These launch terms are public-safe placeholders and should be reviewed by counsel before production release.

Service

MySLI is a marketplace and workflow platform for human sign language interpreting sessions. Users must use the service respectfully, lawfully, and in a way that protects Deaf users, interpreters, patients, students, employees, and organizations.

Interpreter qualifications

Interpreter eligibility may depend on the client state, session type, specialization, credential verification, identity verification, and professional standards. MySLI may block booking, video launch, payment, or payout when required verification is missing.

Corporate and institutional sessions

For ADA-required corporate, hospital, clinic, school, or workplace sessions, the organization may be responsible for payment. Deaf patients or customers should not be charged by the organization for required interpreting access.

Payments, receipts, and refunds

Payment, cancellation, refund, and payout rules may vary by session type, corporate payer setup, interpreter rate, and billing configuration. MySLI aims to show clear rates before booking and clear receipts after sessions.

No emergency services

MySLI is not an emergency service. If there is an emergency, contact local emergency services directly.
