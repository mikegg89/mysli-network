Organization-paid interpreting

Corporate ADA pilots for human interpreter access.

MySLI is preparing controlled pilots for hospitals, clinics, schools, businesses, architecture and engineering teams, and other organizations that need reliable organization-paid sign language interpreting workflows.

The core promise is simple: the Deaf person should keep interpreter choice whenever possible, and the organization pays for ADA-required access. The Deaf user should be charged $0 for corporate sessions.

Pilot workflows

Reusable QR codes

Print branded QR codes for approved locations, departments, rooms, or shared devices. The QR tells MySLI which organization pays while the Deaf user still chooses from eligible interpreters.

Shared iPad launch

Staff can open the corporate workflow on a dedicated device, ask whether the Deaf person wants help scheduling, and route the session to the corporate payer.

Technical and spatial pilots

Architecture, engineering, manufacturing, product, and research teams can test technical interpreter matching with CAD/spatial collaboration only after state, identity, billing, and readiness gates pass.

What we need from pilot organizations

  • Organization type and primary pilot contact.
  • State, location, department, or room context for expected sessions.
  • Expected monthly session volume and common session categories.
  • Billing readiness and corporate payer contact.
  • Support contact for QR misuse, billing questions, and urgent workflow issues.
  • Written acknowledgement that the Deaf user is charged $0 for organization-paid access.

Safety and compliance gates

MySLI will only show interpreters who pass the client-state eligibility gate and requested specialization match. Corporate billing cannot bypass interpreter credential review, identity checks, state rules, professional standards, or video readiness.

Please do not send private medical, student, employee, legal, insurance, or payment card details through the early-access form.
